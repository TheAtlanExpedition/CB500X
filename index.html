My Expeditions On The CB500X

My first ride on dirt and with street tyres no less.

Blackdown Tablelands, QLD,
It starts with sealed roads over cattle grates into open flat fields with cows roaming freely then to the curving road up the mountain, from there it quickly turns into a subtropical scene that seems to only exist on the side of the mountain and in the gully where the rock pool is. Riding up the mountain I came up to a clearing on the side of the road with a nice view over the fields, seemed like the perfect place for a pitstop to enjoy the view.

At the top of the mountain was an incredible view a short walk from the rest stop just before the road turned to dirt. The view is so unexpected that it can give one a new perspective. Usually when you get a view from the mountain top it never shows the great view of the mountain itself and its place in the land below, you'd need a drone for a view like this.

Heading off the sealed road for the first time was intimidating to start with but I'd already gotten some good advice online about standing, essentially, "stand when you don't want to and sit when it's easy". The street tyres and pressures didn't help with the corrugation. I was dodging potholes and slowing to a crawl around corners to avoid slipping on the gravel. Keep in mind this was before I'd gotten any protection for me or the bike.
